CASE R2-13
INCIDENT: Anchor Relay Offset Recalibration

SUMMARY
The anchor offset table was recalibrated by hand. The effect spread across the timeline network.

INCIDENT TIMELINE
20:03:01 | First abnormal reading: anchor drift of 370 ms across the relay chain
20:03:18 | Monitoring reports a sustained inconsistency
20:03:57 | Automatic reconciliation fails to restore the baseline
20:05:08 | Cross-timeline results leave the normal operating range

ORIGIN ANALYSIS
The first abnormal reading originates at Anchor Relay AR-7.
It matches change CHG-8808 applied at 20:02:54 (recalibration of temporal anchor offsets).
No other change within the incident window touched the anchor offset table.

OBSERVATIONS
A mitigation hold was applied at Anchor Relay AR-7 at 20:03:46; it did not restore the baseline.
Baseline checks before the incident were within their normal range.
Operators on shift reported no scheduled maintenance for this subsystem.

END OF REPORT
