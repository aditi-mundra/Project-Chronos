CASE R2-24
INCIDENT: Transit Buffer Emergency Flush

SUMMARY
The transit buffer was flushed outside its maintenance window. The effect spread across the timeline network.

INCIDENT TIMELINE
18:45:31 | First abnormal reading: transit buffer overrun of 328 MB
18:45:49 | Monitoring reports a sustained inconsistency
18:46:14 | Automatic reconciliation fails to restore the baseline
18:47:12 | Cross-timeline results leave the normal operating range

ORIGIN ANALYSIS
The first abnormal reading cannot be traced to a single source. Candidate nodes: Transit Buffer TB-1 and Transit Buffer TB-7.
No change record could be matched to the reading with certainty.

OBSERVATIONS
A mitigation hold was applied at Transit Buffer TB-1 at 18:46:01; it did not restore the baseline.
Baseline checks before the incident were within their normal range.
Operators on shift reported no scheduled maintenance for this subsystem.

END OF REPORT
