CASE R2-08
INCIDENT: Transit Buffer Emergency Flush

SUMMARY
The transit buffer was flushed outside its maintenance window. The effect spread across the timeline network.

INCIDENT TIMELINE
21:12:40 | First abnormal reading: transit buffer overrun of 196 MB
21:12:57 | Monitoring reports a sustained inconsistency
21:13:47 | Automatic reconciliation fails to restore the baseline
21:14:25 | Cross-timeline results leave the normal operating range

ORIGIN ANALYSIS
The first abnormal reading originates at Transit Buffer TB-5.
It matches change CHG-3571 applied at 21:12:30 (forced flush of the transit buffer).
No other change within the incident window touched the transit buffer content.

OBSERVATIONS
A mitigation hold was applied at Transit Buffer TB-5 at 21:13:33; it did not restore the baseline.
Baseline checks before the incident were within their normal range.
Operators on shift reported no scheduled maintenance for this subsystem.
