INCIDENT: Anchor Relay Offset Recalibration

SUMMARY
The anchor offset table was recalibrated by hand. The effect spread across the timeline network.

INCIDENT TIMELINE
23:11:19 | First abnormal reading: anchor drift of 211 ms across the relay chain
23:11:41 | Monitoring reports a sustained inconsistency
23:11:59 | Automatic reconciliation fails to restore the baseline
23:13:10 | Cross-timeline results leave the normal operating range

ORIGIN ANALYSIS
The first abnormal reading originates at Anchor Relay AR-5.
It matches change CHG-6068 applied at 23:11:10 (recalibration of temporal anchor offsets).
No other change within the incident window touched the anchor offset table.

OBSERVATIONS
A mitigation hold was applied at Anchor Relay AR-5 at 23:12:07; it did not restore the baseline.
Baseline checks before the incident were within their normal range.
Operators on shift reported no scheduled maintenance for this subsystem.
