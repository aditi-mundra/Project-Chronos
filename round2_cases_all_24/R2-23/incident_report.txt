CASE R2-23
INCIDENT: Cross-Timeline Sync Governor Shutdown

SUMMARY
The cross-timeline sync governor was halted. The effect spread across the timeline network.

INCIDENT TIMELINE
21:22:31 | First abnormal reading: desynchronisation of 10 timeline channels
21:22:53 | Monitoring reports a sustained inconsistency
21:23:36 | Automatic reconciliation fails to restore the baseline
21:24:50 | Cross-timeline results leave the normal operating range

ORIGIN ANALYSIS
The first abnormal reading cannot be traced to a single source. Candidate nodes: Sync Governor SG-2 and Sync Governor SG-5.
No change record could be matched to the reading with certainty.

OBSERVATIONS
A mitigation hold was applied at Sync Governor SG-2 at 21:23:12; it did not restore the baseline.
Baseline checks before the incident were within their normal range.
Operators on shift reported no scheduled maintenance for this subsystem.

END OF REPORT
