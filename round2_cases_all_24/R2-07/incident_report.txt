INCIDENT REPORT
Case R2-07 | Incident: Cross-Timeline Sync Shutdown

WHAT HAPPENED
The cross-timeline sync controller was stopped. The effect spread across the timeline network.

TIMELINE OF EVENTS
18:26:53 | First abnormal reading: 3 timeline channels out of sync
18:27:11 | Monitoring reports a sustained inconsistency
18:27:40 | Automatic recovery fails to restore normal readings
18:28:36 | Results across timelines leave the normal range

WHERE IT STARTED
The first abnormal reading came from Sync Controller SG-4.
It lines up with Change #2537 made at 18:26:46 (shutdown of the cross-timeline sync controller).
No other change in this period touched the sync controller state.

OTHER NOTES
A mitigation hold was applied at Sync Controller SG-4 at 18:27:57; it did not restore normal readings.
Readings before the incident were in their normal range.
Operators on shift reported no scheduled maintenance for this system.

END OF REPORT
